# SWOT execution plan

## 0–30 days: establish the evidence loop

- Publish Cite Watch Pulse methodology and a ten-question pilot with a public observation table.
- Create a single disambiguated identity string: **Mason Nguyen — GEO Research & AI Systems**. Use it consistently on the site, LinkedIn, GitHub, speaker bios, and bylines.
- Complete a factual entity record only where eligibility and third-party sourcing exist; do not create or edit encyclopedic entries to promote the site.
- Add a newsletter and booking/contact destination only after selecting the provider, consent copy, and commercial owner. Do not collect emails through an unowned endpoint.

## 31–60 days: earn corroboration

- Publish one co-authored research note or a case record with a named reviewer and disclosed evidence scope.
- Pitch three practitioner podcasts and two editorial contributions using the method or pilot—not a generic GEO pitch.
- Turn the pilot’s raw table, methodology, and corrections log into a stable citation target.

## 61–90 days: compound distribution

- Repeat the study with the same declared conditions and publish a source-drift change log.
- Release one usable template: source-audit spreadsheet, evidence-lineage checklist, or entity-conflict worksheet.
- Review Search Console query/page data once access is restored; refresh pages with demonstrated demand before expanding the editorial map.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- Do not promise rankings, citations, AI-answer inclusion, or commercial outcomes.
- Do not use fabricated case studies, unverifiable testimonials, or self-authored claims as third-party proof.
- Treat external citations as earned corroboration, never as an entitlement.
