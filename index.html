07 / FAQ

Key terms, kept precise.

The homepage preserves its accessible FAQ content while dropping the old performance-dashboard treatment.

What is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)?

GEO is the practice of improving how digital content, entity signals, and schema help AI systems identify and represent a source. It complements search strategy; outcomes depend on source quality, corroboration, retrieval behavior, and the questions being measured.

What does Mason Nguyen specialize in?

Mason Nguyen researches GEO strategy, agentic AI infrastructure, and the ARM Agent Protocol: a governance architecture that uses the primitives Perceive, Reason, Plan, Execute, and Reflect.

How does GEO differ from traditional SEO?

SEO and GEO work with overlapping source and content disciplines but have distinct representation and retrieval contexts. The relevant method depends on a source, audience, query set, and the public surfaces available for inspection.

What is Share of Model (SoM)?

Share of Model is a proposed measurement concept for examining how a defined entity appears across a declared query set and model set. Any observation requires its prompt, date, model, evidence, and review boundary.
