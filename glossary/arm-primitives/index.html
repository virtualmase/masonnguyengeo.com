ARM Primitives
AP  /ˈɑːrm ˈprɪm.ɪ.tɪvz/  ·  noun
The five foundational building blocks that enable sovereign AI infrastructure: Entity Resolution, Signal Architecture, Citation Networks, Retrieval Design, and Momentum Maintenance. Each primitive is necessary but not sufficient. the primitives must be deployed as an integrated system. ARM Primitives are the atomic units of the ARM Framework, the methodology for building machine-readable presence that AI language models can verify, trust, and cite.
Distinguished from the ARM Framework itself, which is the methodology (Authority, Retrieval, Mandate). The Primitives are the bricks. The Framework is the architecture. The Primitives define what you build. The Framework defines how you assemble them.
The Goal

What is sovereign AI infrastructure?

Sovereign AI infrastructure is the machine-readable signal system that makes an entity independently verifiable by AI models. not dependent on any single platform, search engine, or intermediary. An entity with sovereign infrastructure can be cited by any AI model that encounters it, because its verification graph exists independently of any one system's index.

The opposite of sovereign is platform-dependent. A brand that exists only on Instagram, only in Google's index, or only in one LLM's training data is not sovereign. When the platform changes its algorithm, the brand's visibility disappears. Sovereign infrastructure means the entity is resolvable, verifiable, and citable across any system. because the signals exist in the open, in machine-readable form, across independent surfaces.

The ARM Primitives are how you build it. Five blocks. One system.

The Primitives

Five blocks, one system

Each primitive addresses a specific failure mode. Skip one and the system develops a specific vulnerability. Deploy all five and the entity becomes sovereign. verifiable, citable, and durable across any AI system.

01
Entity Resolution Entity Authority →
Can the AI model identify the entity unambiguously? Schema.org JSON-LD with Person or Organization types. sameAs arrays linking to Wikidata, LinkedIn, GitHub. Consistent naming across every surface. Without resolution, citation is impossible. the model cannot cite what it cannot identify. This is the entry point for the entire system.
02
Signal Architecture Signal Architecture →
Can the AI model verify the entity's claims? The structured system of machine-readable signals: schema, knowledge graph registration, llms.txt, content structure. Signal Architecture is the infrastructure layer. it determines whether the model can traverse the entity's verification graph and confirm what the entity claims to be.
03
Citation Networks
Do independent sources confirm the entity's claims? Third-party references from authoritative publications. editorial mentions, not paid placements. The Princeton GEO research showed that structured citation can boost AI visibility by up to 40%. Without this primitive, Entity Resolution and Signal Architecture are self-assertion. Citation Networks are the proof.
04
Retrieval Design
Can AI systems find the entity's content when constructing answers? llms.txt at the domain root. robots.txt configured for AI crawlers (open for citation, closed for training). Machine-readable content structure: direct answers in first sentences, FAQ-formatted Q&A, stable semantic URLs. This primitive controls whether the infrastructure is visible to the systems that would cite it.
05
Momentum Maintenance Signal Decay →
Is the signal environment current? Ongoing publication, content updates, schema refreshes, citation network expansion. Without momentum, the other four primitives erode through Signal Decay. Momentum Maintenance is the maintenance cost of sovereign AI infrastructure. the price of sustained Share of Model.

The Mapping

How the Primitives assemble into the ARM Framework

The ARM Framework defines three pillars: Authority, Retrieval, Mandate. The Primitives map to these pillars. some primitives serve multiple pillars, but each has a primary home.

Authority
Entity Resolution. the model can identify you
Citation Networks. independent sources confirm you
Output: Entity Authority
+
Retrieval
Signal Architecture. the model can verify you
Retrieval Design. the model can find you
Output: Citation probability in AI responses
Momentum
Momentum Maintenance spans all three pillars. Without it, Authority erodes (stale entity signals), Retrieval erodes (outdated content), and the entire system decays. Momentum is not a pillar. it is the force that keeps the pillars standing.

FAQ

Common questions about ARM Primitives

What are the ARM Primitives?
The ARM Primitives are five foundational building blocks for sovereign AI infrastructure: Entity Resolution, Signal Architecture, Citation Networks, Retrieval Design, and Momentum Maintenance. Each primitive is necessary but not sufficient. they must be deployed as an integrated system.
How do ARM Primitives relate to the ARM Framework?
The ARM Framework (Authority, Retrieval, Mandate) is the methodology. The ARM Primitives are the atomic units that the methodology assembles. Authority is built from Entity Resolution and Citation Networks. Retrieval is built from Signal Architecture and Retrieval Design. Mandate is built from Momentum Maintenance. The framework is the architecture; the primitives are the bricks.
What is sovereign AI infrastructure?
Sovereign AI infrastructure is the machine-readable signal system that makes an entity independently verifiable by AI models. not dependent on any single platform, search engine, or intermediary. An entity with sovereign infrastructure can be cited by any AI model that encounters it, because its verification graph exists independently of any one system's index.
Why must the primitives be deployed together?
Each primitive addresses a different failure mode. Entity Resolution without Citation Networks is self-assertion. Citation Networks without Signal Architecture are disconnected references. Signal Architecture without Retrieval Design is invisible infrastructure. Retrieval Design without Momentum Maintenance decays. Only the full stack produces compounding Entity Authority and sustained Share of Model.
What happens if you skip a primitive?
Skipping a primitive creates a specific vulnerability. Skip Entity Resolution and the model cannot identify you. Skip Signal Architecture and the model cannot verify your claims. Skip Citation Networks and your claims are self-asserted. Skip Retrieval Design and your content is unfindable. Skip Momentum Maintenance and all four erode through Signal Decay.
