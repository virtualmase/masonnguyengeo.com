Entity Authority (EA)
The doorman knows your face.

Domain Authority is how many people have your business card. Entity Authority is whether the doorman waves you through without checking the list. One is quantity. The other is recognition. AI models are the doorman. They don't read business cards.

Entity Authority
EA  /ˈɛn.tɪ.ti əˈθɒr.ɪ.ti/  ·  noun
The degree to which AI language models trust, resolve, and accurately represent a named entity in generated responses. Entity Authority is the AI-era successor to Domain Authority. it measures not the strength of a domain's link profile, but the strength of an entity's machine-readable presence across the signals AI models use to construct answers.
Distinguished from Domain Authority (Moz), which quantifies a domain's ranking power based on backlink quantity and quality. Entity Authority quantifies an entity's citation probability based on schema completeness, knowledge graph registration, citation network density, and signal freshness. A brand can have high Domain Authority and low Entity Authority. Visible in search. Invisible in AI answers.
The Shift

Domain Authority answered "who ranks?" Entity Authority answers "who gets cited?"

Domain Authority was built for a world where Google's PageRank algorithm determined visibility. The metric measured the quantity and quality of backlinks pointing at a domain. More links from authoritative domains meant higher rankings. It was a link economy.

AI language models do not run PageRank. They resolve entities. named things with properties, relationships, and provenance. and cite them in generated responses. The question is not "how many domains link to you?" but "can the machine verify who you are, what you know, and whether independent sources confirm it?"

This is why a brand with Domain Authority 60 can be invisible in ChatGPT responses while a niche expert with DA 25 appears in every relevant answer. The first has link mass. The second has entity clarity, consistent naming, and a citation network that AI models can verify.

The Architecture

Four layers that build Entity Authority

Entity Authority is not a single signal. It is the composite output of four layers, each building on the previous one. Skip a layer and the structure collapses.

01
Entity Resolution
Can the AI model identify the entity unambiguously? This requires Schema.org JSON-LD with Person or Organization types, consistent naming across all surfaces, and sameAs arrays linking to Wikidata, LinkedIn, GitHub, and other identity surfaces. Without resolution, citation is impossible. Entity graph architecture →
02
E-E-A-T Signals
Does the entity demonstrate Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness? Google's quality rater guidelines established E-E-A-T as the credibility framework. AI systems use the same signals. author credentials, publisher reputation, first-hand experience indicators, and factual accuracy. Search Engine Land's analysis of entities and E-A-T documents how entity-level signals now feed authority evaluation.
03
Citation Network
Do independent, authoritative sources reference the entity? When an AI model has low-confidence knowledge of an entity, it triangulates using third-party mentions. Editorial citations from authoritative publications confirm entity claims. A Quattr analysis found that structured data, consistent brand information, and third-party mentions are what teach AI models who you are and whether you can be trusted.
04
Signal Freshness
Is the entity's signal environment current? AI systems weight recency. An entity that published strong signals two years ago and went silent is experiencing Signal Decay. Freshness is the maintenance cost of Entity Authority. without it, the other three layers erode.

Skip a layer and the structure collapses. Resolution without citations is self-assertion. Citations without architecture are disconnected references. Architecture without freshness is a monument to what you used to be. All four. Or none of it holds.

Comparison

Entity Authority vs. Domain Authority. different economies

Both metrics measure "how visible is this thing?" But they measure visibility on entirely different surfaces, through different mechanisms, with different leverage points.

Domain Authority
Measures: link profile strength of a domain
Surface: search engine result pages
Signal: backlinks, referring domains, link quality
Scale: domain-level (not entity-level)
Can be influenced: yes (link building, outreach)
Owner: Moz (proprietary metric)
↔
Different economies
Entity Authority
Measures: machine-readable signal strength of an entity
Surface: AI-generated responses (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini)
Signal: schema, knowledge graph, citation network, freshness
Scale: entity-level (person, org, concept)
Can be influenced: yes (schema, registration, content)
Owner: open (no proprietary metric yet. Signal Score™ is one approach)

The infrastructure overlaps. The optimization targets diverge. A brand that has invested years in SEO has a strong foundation. But only if the entity layer is built on top of it. Without that layer, the SEO investment produces rankings. Not citations.

The Bridge

Domain Authority feeds Entity Authority. but does not guarantee it

A domain with strong backlinks has a head start. Those backlinks are citations. If the domain's content is structured with proper schema, the entity associated with the domain inherits some of that authority. This is why GEO and SEO share infrastructure. the link profile that built Domain Authority also contributes to Entity Authority when it's properly structured.

But the bridge only works in one direction. A brand can transfer Domain Authority into Entity Authority through schema and content architecture. A brand cannot transfer Entity Authority into Domain Authority. AI citations do not produce backlinks.

This is the strategic insight: SEO investment compounds into GEO outcomes, but only if the entity architecture layer is built. Without it, Domain Authority stays Domain Authority. invisible to the models that users increasingly trust for recommendations.

The Work

Five steps to build Entity Authority from zero

For new entities. startups, personal brands, new product categories. Entity Authority starts at zero. The build sequence is non-negotiable. Each step enables the next.

01
Define the entity in schema
Publish JSON-LD with Person or Organization type. Include name, description, url, image, sameAs array linking to all identity surfaces, and knowsAbout array listing areas of expertise. This is the entry point. without it, the entity does not exist in machine-readable form.
02
Register in the knowledge graph
Create a Wikidata entry. Link it via sameAs in your schema. This gives AI models a canonical reference point for resolving the entity. one they trust more than self-authored schema alone.
03
Publish authoritative content
Create content that demonstrates expertise: original research, frameworks, definitions. The Share of Model canonical definition is an example. it establishes the entity as the origin point for a concept AI models can reference.
04
Build the citation network
Earn editorial mentions from authoritative third-party sources. The Princeton GEO research demonstrated that structured citation can boost AI visibility by up to 40%. Each new citation strengthens the entity's verification graph.
05
Maintain freshness
Publish consistently. Update schema as the entity evolves. Measure Share of Model monthly. Without freshness, Entity Authority erodes through Signal Decay.

Five steps. Sequential. Non-negotiable. Each enables the next. A brand that tries to build citation networks before defining the entity in schema is asking people to verify something the machines cannot identify. Start at zero. Build in order.

FAQ

Common questions about Entity Authority

What is Entity Authority?
Entity Authority is the degree to which AI language models trust, resolve, and accurately represent a named entity in generated responses. It measures the strength of an entity's machine-readable presence. schema, knowledge graph registration, citation networks. rather than the link profile of a domain.
How is Entity Authority different from Domain Authority?
Domain Authority (Moz) measures the link-based ranking strength of a domain in traditional search. Entity Authority measures the machine-readable signal strength of an entity across AI systems. A brand can have high Domain Authority but low Entity Authority. strong in search rankings, invisible in AI-generated answers. The metrics measure different surfaces.
How does E-E-A-T relate to Entity Authority?
Google's E-E-A-T framework (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) is the quality signal layer that feeds Entity Authority. E-E-A-T tells quality raters and AI systems whether a source is credible. Entity Authority is the outcome. whether AI models actually cite the entity. E-E-A-T is the input; Entity Authority is the output.
How do you build Entity Authority?
Build Entity Authority through: structured data (Schema.org JSON-LD with Person/Organization types), knowledge graph registration (Wikidata, sameAs arrays), consistent entity naming across all surfaces, third-party citation networks from authoritative sources, and signal freshness through ongoing publication. The ARM Framework provides the systematic build sequence.
Can you buy Entity Authority?
No. Entity Authority cannot be bought through media spend or paid links. It is earned through verifiable entity architecture, consistent third-party citations, and machine-readable signals that AI models can independently verify. Paid placements do not contribute to Entity Authority because AI systems discount signals that can be purchased.
